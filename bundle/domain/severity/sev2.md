# SEV2

SEV2 means: Major impact on a key experience. It is one of the three closed incident severities (SEV1, SEV2, SEV3) in the severity scheme; see [severity scheme](scheme.md).

Surface forms seen in chat and free text, "SEV 2" and "SEV-2", all mean SEV2.[^architecture]

[^architecture]: Severity scheme as recorded in the e2ekg architecture document.
