# Policy: hypotheses carry evidence

Hypotheses can be wrong, so every hypothesis carries its evidence and its causal chain. A reviewer can then check the claim and a retracted guess stays visible as retracted.[^design]

[^design]: e2ekg design, flywheel and chat claims.
